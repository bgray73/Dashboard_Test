## Handoff Rules

- Do not restart from scratch without reviewing the supplied branch/worktree and evidence.
- Verify important prior findings before relying on them.
- Do not repeat failed approaches unless new evidence justifies doing so.
- Keep changes scoped to the stated objective and acceptance criteria.
- Do not modify `main` directly.
- Never include secrets or credentials in the handoff.
- Never claim tests or commands succeeded unless they were actually executed.
